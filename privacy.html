Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

DMT Radio Records ("the tool") is a private desktop tool used only by the DMT Radio Records account owner to upload the channel's own videos to its own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

What the tool accesses

  • TikTok: the signed-in account's basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID), used only to confirm which account an upload goes to, and permission to upload videos to that account's drafts inbox.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the signed-in channel as private videos.

Where it is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the account owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's and Google's own APIs. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Other people's data

The tool does not collect, read or store data about any other TikTok or YouTube users.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok and Google account settings; deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
